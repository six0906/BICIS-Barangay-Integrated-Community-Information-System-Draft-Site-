Privacy Terms & Agreement

Your Information, Handled Responsibly

This Privacy Terms and Agreement outlines how the Barangay Integrated Community Information System (BICIS) handles personal information in accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

1. Why BICIS Requires an Email Address

When registering for a resident account, BICIS requests a valid email address as a unique account identifier and direct communication channel for account security and essential system updates.

2. Registration and Login

Your email address is used to register your account profile and authenticate your sign-in requests when connected to the barangay's authorized server backend.

3. Account Verification

When enabled by the system administrators, your email address may be used to verify account ownership and reduce unauthorized or duplicate registrations.

4. Password Recovery

If you forget your password, your registered email address may be used to send password-reset instructions or verification links so you can securely regain access to your account.

5. Essential Account-Related Notifications

BICIS may use your email address strictly for essential account-related notices (such as security alerts, verification updates, or requested service status notices). Email addresses are not used for unrelated marketing or commercial purposes without an appropriate legal basis and transparent user consent.

6. Storage and Protection of Personal Information

Personal data submitted through a deployed BICIS backend should be stored with appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards (such as encrypted transport and hashed passwords). This frontend interface never stores real passwords in browser localStorage. Actual server-side retention and security controls are maintained by the designated Barangay Dimagiba system administrators.

7. Requesting Assistance Regarding Your Information

Under RA 10173, data subjects have rights to be informed, access, object, rectify inaccurate records, or request deletion/blocking of personal information subject to applicable law and official barangay record-keeping rules.

8. Contacting the BICIS Administrator

For questions, correction requests, or privacy concerns, please contact the designated BICIS Administrator or Barangay Data Protection Officer at the Barangay Dimagiba Hall, Legazpi City, Albay, or through the official administrator contact channels published upon live system deployment.
