Privacy Terms & Agreement

Your Information, Handled Responsibly.

How the Barangay Integrated Community Information System (BICIS) collects, uses, and protects your personal information in alignment with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

1. Why BICIS Requires an Email Address

BICIS requests an email address during account registration so each resident account has a unique identifier and a dependable communication channel for account access, security, and essential notices. We avoid collecting unnecessary personal details beyond what the barangay system requires.

2. Registration and Login

Your email address and credentials are used to create your resident profile and authenticate sign-in attempts when BICIS is connected to the authorized barangay backend server.

3. Account Verification

Where enabled by system administrators, your email address may be used to verify account ownership and reduce unauthorized, mistaken, or duplicate account registrations.

4. Password Recovery

If you are unable to recall your password, your registered email address may be used to send password-recovery instructions or reset links so you can securely restore account access.

5. Essential Account-Related Notifications

Your email address may be used strictly for essential account-related notifications—such as security updates, verification confirmations, or requested community account notices. BICIS does not use resident email addresses for unrelated or commercial purposes without an appropriate legal basis and transparent consent.

6. Storage and Protection of Personal Information

This frontend interface never stores real passwords in browser localStorage. When deployed on a live server (such as PHP and MySQL), the designated Barangay Dimagiba administrators are responsible for implementing organizational, physical, and technical security measures consistent with RA 10173, including secure password hashing, restricted administrative access, and encrypted transport where configured.

7. Requesting Assistance Regarding Your Information

Consistent with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), residents have the right to be informed about how their personal data is processed, to request access to their records, to ask for corrections of inaccurate or outdated information, and to seek removal or blocking where appropriate under applicable law and official barangay retention policies.

8. Contacting the BICIS Administrator

For privacy inquiries, data correction requests, or concerns regarding account handling, please contact the designated BICIS Administrator or Barangay Data Protection Officer for Barangay Dimagiba, Legazpi City, Albay, Philippines through the official barangay hall channels published by the project administrators.
