Barangay Dimagiba · Legazpi City, Albay

Community Services & Public Information

Explore the four core sections of BICIS: the central community information platform, Disaster Risk Reduction and Management (DRRM), Gender and Development (GAD), and Social Welfare Services.

01 · Central Community Platform

Barangay Integrated Community Information System (BICIS)

Your central destination for accessible barangay information, essential services, community updates, and public resources in Barangay Dimagiba.

Explore Barangay Dimagiba Map

Key Public Resources

  • Centralized barangay announcements, assembly schedules, and public advisories.
  • Accessible navigation to DRRM, GAD, and Social Welfare desks.
  • Resident account registration and transparent privacy controls under RA 10173.
  • Interactive illustrated community map highlighting key barangay service hubs.
02 · Preparedness & Safety

Disaster Risk Reduction and Management (DRRM)

Stay informed, prepared, and aware through disaster preparedness information, emergency announcements, safety guidelines, and community risk awareness.

Contact DRRM Desk

Preparedness & Safety Focus

  • Household emergency go-bag reminders: clean water, first aid, flashlight, radio, and vital documents.
  • Community risk awareness for typhoons, heavy rainfall, flooding, and volcanic or seismic advisories relevant to Albay.
  • Clear safety guidelines and coordination notices issued by official barangay and city DRRM authorities.
  • Pre-emptive evacuation readiness and designated assembly zone guidance.
03 · Equality & Inclusivity

Gender and Development (GAD)

Promoting equality, inclusivity, awareness, and accessible information about gender-related programs and community initiatives.

Inquire About GAD Programs

Program & Awareness Priorities

  • Information on inclusive barangay orientations, community workshops, and equality advocacy.
  • Safe-space awareness and referral guidance for residents seeking confidential community support.
  • Accessible participation opportunities for women, youth, and underrepresented community members.
  • Transparent sharing of Barangay GAD activity calendars and public initiatives.
04 · Community Assistance

Social Welfare Services

Discover community assistance programs, social services, available support, and important information for residents and families.

Create Resident Account

Resident & Family Support

  • Information on community aid schedules, eligibility guidance, and barangay referral procedures.
  • Support awareness for senior citizens, persons with disabilities, solo parents, and vulnerable households.
  • Clear checklists of standard documents requested by barangay welfare desks.
  • Direct links to community inquiry channels for resident follow-up.
