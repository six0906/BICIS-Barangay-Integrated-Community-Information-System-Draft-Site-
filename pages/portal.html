How BICIS Works Together

All four modules share one Resident Registry so staff never have to enter the same household twice.

1. Resident Registry

Central Records

Register and update Household, Family, and Resident records in one place, including puroks, ages, and sector tags (Senior Citizen, PWD, 4Ps, Solo Parent, Pregnant, Lactating, Infant).

Open Resident Registry

2. GAD-CARE

Women & Family Care

Tag eligible residents from the Registry, track needs assessments, manage maternity and hygiene supplies, and release kits without duplicate claims or negative stock.

Open GAD-CARE

3. GABAY

Social Welfare Aid

Enroll Registry households into barangay programs, review urgent medical and burial requests first, prevent duplicate aid releases, and print Certificates of Indigency.

Open GABAY

4. HANDA+

Disaster Readiness

Prepare Priority Rescue Lists using shared barangay records, record evacuation check-ins, prevent duplicate relief claims, and share public safety alerts with residents.

Open HANDA+
