Privacy Terms & Agreement

Your Information, Handled Responsibly

This Privacy Terms and Agreement explains how the Barangay Integrated Community Information System (BICIS) collects, uses, and safeguards your account information in accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

1. Why BICIS Requires an Email Address

An email address is requested to uniquely identify your resident account and provide a reliable contact channel for essential account actions.

2. Registration and Login

Your email address is used during registration and sign-in authentication when BICIS is connected to its authorized barangay server backend.

3. Account Verification

Your email address may be used to verify that you own the submitted address and to help prevent duplicate or unauthorized registrations.

4. Password Recovery

If you forget your password, your registered email address may be used to deliver password-recovery instructions or reset links.

5. Essential Account-Related Notifications

Your email is used strictly for essential account-related notices (security alerts, verification updates, or requested community account updates) and not for unrelated purposes without legal basis and transparent consent.

6. Storage and Protection of Personal Information

This static frontend does not store real passwords in localStorage. Upon live deployment on a barangay server (such as PHP/MySQL under XAMPP), administrators are responsible for maintaining appropriate technical, physical, and organizational safeguards, including secure password hashing and access controls.

7. How to Request Assistance Regarding Your Information

Under RA 10173, you have the right to inquire about, access, correct, or request removal of inaccurate or unnecessary personal information, subject to lawful barangay record-keeping requirements.

8. Contacting the BICIS Administrator

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the designated BICIS Administrator or Barangay Data Protection Officer at Barangay Dimagiba, Legazpi City, Albay.
