About the Platform

Keep You and Your Community Safe

The Barangay Integrated Community Information System (BICIS) is built to give residents of Barangay Dimagiba, Legazpi City, Albay clear, equitable, and dependable access to public community information.

Mission & Purpose

Accessible Civic Information Without Barriers

Public information works best when ordinary residents can read, navigate, and trust it on any phone, tablet, or desktop computer. BICIS brings together barangay updates, Disaster Risk Reduction and Management (DRRM), Gender and Development (GAD), and Social Welfare Services in one structured portal.

Built with lightweight HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript, the frontend stays fast, maintainable, and ready to connect with local XAMPP / PHP / MySQL infrastructure.

Original BICIS Identity

Symbolism Behind the Emblem

The protective shield-pin silhouette unites community safety with local geography; the inner arcs represent connected residents; and the central orange beacon symbolizes accessible barangay information.
