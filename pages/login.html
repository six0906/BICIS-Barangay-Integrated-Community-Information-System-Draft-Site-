Privacy Terms & Agreement

Your Information, Handled Responsibly

In accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), BICIS explains how resident account information is handled:

1. Why an Email Address Is Required

Your email address serves as your unique account identifier and secure contact channel for account management.

2. Registration & Login

Used to create and authenticate your account when connected to the official barangay server.

3. Account Verification

May be used to confirm ownership of your registration and prevent unauthorized account creation.

4. Password Recovery

Used to verify identity and send reset instructions if you lose access to your password.

5. Essential Account-Related Notifications

Limited to essential account and requested service notifications; never used for unrelated purposes without legal basis and transparent consent.

6. Storage & Protection

No real passwords are stored in browser localStorage. Server-side protection is administered by the deployed Barangay Dimagiba backend using appropriate technical and organizational safeguards.

7. Data Subject Assistance

You may request access, correction, or assistance regarding your personal data under RA 10173.

8. Contacting the BICIS Administrator

Reach out to the designated BICIS Administrator at Barangay Dimagiba, Legazpi City, Albay regarding any privacy concerns.
